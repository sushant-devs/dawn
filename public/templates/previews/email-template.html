Important Safety Information

CONTRAINDICATIONS: None.

WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS: Neutropenia and hepatotoxicity have been reported with BREXIVA. Monitor complete blood counts prior to and during treatment. Monitor liver function tests. Dose modifications may be required.

ADVERSE REACTIONS: The most common adverse reactions (≥10%) were neutropenia, nausea, fatigue, diarrhea, and alopecia.

DRUG INTERACTIONS: Strong CYP3A inhibitors may increase Brexiva exposure. Avoid concomitant use or reduce Brexiva dose.
